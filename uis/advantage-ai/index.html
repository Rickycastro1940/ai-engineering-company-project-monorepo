Before we build

Clear answers.

A real engineer. A focused conversation about your workflow.

Do we need to replace our current software?

The focus is connecting to the software you already use. We assess APIs, data access and integration constraints during the evaluation, before agreeing on a scope.

Where should we start?

Choose one repetitive workflow with clear inputs and outputs: invoice intake, record synchronization or a recurring reporting handoff. The evaluation helps determine feasibility and what to measure.

What happens after I request an evaluation?

An engineer reviews your request and replies within one business day. We clarify the workflow and arrange next steps together. The form requests a conversation; it does not reserve a calendar slot.

Who owns the finished system?

You retain the code, data and documentation. Engagements use defined milestones, with scope and pricing agreed before implementation.
