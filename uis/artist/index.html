01 — About

Between the bakery door and the stage.

Vera grew up behind a family bakery counter in Getsemaní, sketching the street from the doorway between customers. She studied painting in Medellín and started singing the titles of unfinished canvases so she would remember the color mixes.

The habit became the practice. Each exhibition opens with a twenty-minute set, then the room stays open so people can stand with the work. The songs are short. The paintings are not.

02 — Career

The work keeps moving.

  1. 2026

    Night Market, held

    Solo exhibition at Casa del Fuego, Medellín. Twelve canvases and one live opening set recorded in the gallery after hours.

  2. 2025

    Salt Room residency

    A month in Miami painting harbor light and recording four songs in a rented tile studio near the river.

  3. 2024

    Afterglow

    Duo show with printmaker Ren Vega at Taller Norte, Bogotá. Shared a wall of night drawings and a single printed lyric sheet.

  4. 2022

    Low Lantern

    First solo exhibition at Galería Marea, Cartagena. Eight small oils and a song written for the opening only.
